Our Story

Born from Tradition,
Crafted for Today.

Modakghar is a boutique home kitchen crafting premium handmade modaks. Based in Dombivli, Thane, we preserve Maharashtra's most cherished sweet tradition while reimagining it for modern palates.

Every modak is individually shaped by hand using pure, natural ingredients and made fresh after every order — ensuring unmatched texture, aroma, and authentic taste.

From the warmth of a classic Ukadiche Modak to the rich indulgence of Ferrero Rocher and Alphonso Mango creations — we craft sweet memories for your celebrations.
